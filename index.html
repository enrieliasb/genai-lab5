Paper-based packaging

Understand the published sustainability approach before the specialist call.

The Smurfit Westrock Sustainability Guide is a student concept for prospective packaging customers. It explains how the company describes its sustainability approach, then hands quote and design questions to Smurfit Westrock’s own inquiry forms.

What the company has published

These three points are the briefing. Each one keeps the limit that belongs with it. The longer version is on the concept page.

Strategy

3 pillars

Planet. People and Communities. Impactful Business. That is how Smurfit Westrock describes its sustainability strategy.

Target, not a result

28%

A 2030 target to reduce Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse-gas emissions versus a 2019 baseline, announced in May 2026 with the 2025 Sustainability Report. Not an achieved result. Not a Scope 3 goal.

Materials

Circular

A model using renewable, recyclable, and recycled materials for paper-based packaging. Not a promise that every package is recyclable where you live.

Interactive

Circularity Journey

Pick a paper-based package, then click each stage. The motion illustrates the circular model Smurfit Westrock describes. It does not certify a pack or predict a local recycling rule.

Stage 1 of 5

Renewable fiber

Smurfit Westrock describes a circular model that uses renewable materials for paper-based packaging. This corrugated shipper begins with fiber that can be grown again. The step illustrates that published model. It is not a claim about a specific forest, certification, or pack.

What this guide will not decide

The conversation stays with published material. Commercial and product-specific answers go to a Smurfit Westrock specialist.

No product carbon number

The guide does not claim savings for a package, an order, or a lane.

No price or order status

It does not quote, promise a lead time, or look up an order.

No local recyclability promise

Paper-based does not mean every package is collected everywhere.
